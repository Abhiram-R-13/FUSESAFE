# FUSESAFE preliminary material selection

## Proposed material
Aluminium alloy 2024-T3.

Status: PROVISIONAL educational modelling choice.
Not yet approved as a sheet-specific design material.

## Preliminary properties
Young's modulus: 73100 MPa
Poisson's ratio: 0.33
Tensile yield strength: 345 MPa
Density: 2780 kg/m3

## Source
ASM Aerospace Specification Metals supplier data sheet:
https://aerospacemetals.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/Aluminum-2024-T3.pdf

## Important limitations
The source labels several properties as typical and not for design.
These values are preliminary educational model inputs, not certified
aircraft design allowables.

The source does not establish an applicable minimum yield strength
for our exact sheet thickness, direction and product specification.

Before finalizing the material, confirm:
- Bare or clad sheet.
- Applicable thickness range.
- Material direction and isotropic modelling approximation.
- Property temperature applicability.
- Suitable sheet-specific strength data.

Do not report a verified safety conclusion using this preliminary note.
